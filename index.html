Installation

Five steps.
No electrician needed.

The key is a technique unique to Reinvented Works: flipping the plate smooth-side down to set switch spacing before you tighten anything. Takes about 5 minutes.

  1. 01

    Cut the power

    Flip the breaker for the circuit you're working on. Safety first — always.

  2. 02

    Remove the old plate

    Unscrew and pull off your existing cover plate. Set it aside.

  3. 03

    The key step: flip it smooth-side down

    Lay the Reinvented Works plate face-down flat on the wall over both switches. This lets you slide the switches to the correct horizontal spacing before tightening. This is the most important step — don't skip it.

  4. 04

    Tighten the switch screws

    With the plate still face-down, tighten the mounting screws on both switches to lock them in position.

  5. 05

    Snap it on, restore power

    Flip the plate face-up and press it over both switches — it snaps into place and sits flush. Restore power at the breaker. Done.

To remove: Insert a flathead screwdriver into either of the two release slits on the bottom edge and gently lever up.

FAQ

Common questions.

Does this work with all smart switches?

No — it's designed specifically for decorator-style smart switches with a paddle profile. Confirmed compatible models include Kasa/TP-Link (KS220, KS230, EP25), Treatlife (DS01, DS02), Lesim smart paddle, and G-Home decorator switches. It's primarily optimized for Kasa switches. Not all switch brands will fit — if you're unsure, check the compatibility section above before purchasing.

Will this cover a standard US 2-gang wall box?

Yes. The plate is 128 × 128 mm — sized precisely to cover a standard US 2-gang electrical box with the switches installed side by side.

Do I need any tools to install it?

You'll need a screwdriver to tighten the switch mounting screws during the spacing step — that's it. No tools are needed to attach or remove the cover plate itself. To remove, just use a flathead screwdriver in one of the two bottom release slits.

What are those two slots on the bottom of the plate?

Those are the flathead release slits. There are two of them — spaced about a third from each edge of the plate bottom. Insert a flathead screwdriver into either one and gently lever upward to pop the plate off the wall without damaging it or the switches.

Why does it need to be placed smooth-side down during install?

This is the key installation technique that makes the flush fit possible. By laying the plate face-down over both switches before tightening them, you let the plate itself dictate the exact horizontal position of each switch. When you then flip the plate over and snap it on, both switches align perfectly with the cutouts — no gaps, no adjustments needed.

Can I use this with a single switch?

No — this plate is designed specifically for two smart switches side by side in a 2-gang configuration. It won't fit properly over a single switch.

What material is the plate made from?

Unbreakable polycarbonate with a glossy white finish. Polycarbonate is significantly stronger and more heat-resistant than the standard ABS plastic used by most competitors. It won't yellow or crack over time.

Why don't competitors have flush fit options?

Most cover plates on the market — like those from Bestten and Enerlites — are designed for a single switch and rely on exposed screws and inner mounting brackets. They were never engineered for the two-switch flush configuration. Reinvented Works was built from scratch specifically to solve this gap in the market.
